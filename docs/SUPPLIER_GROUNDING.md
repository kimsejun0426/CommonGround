# Supplier grounding and product scope

Reviewed 2026-11-08. These public examples explain why constraints must be product-specific. They are not CommonGround suppliers, integrated offers, or confirmation that multiple brands can share a production run.

## One concrete supplier example

[Packhelp — Custom Rigid Magnetic Box, SKU ph-432-13658](https://packhelp.com/p/magnetic-box/custom/?sku=ph-432-13658) lists a 20 × 20 × 9 cm external size, solid board construction and quantity choices starting at 120, in bundles of 30. Its [magnetic-box category](https://packhelp.com/packaging/boxes/?i=1&type=magnetic-boxes) explicitly states a 120-unit minimum. The detail page also distinguishes finish, inside colour and optional embellishments.

This is evidence that a particular construction can carry a particular minimum and ordering increment. It does not establish compatibility between different artwork, label or sleeve variants. No posted price has been imported into the demo.

## Existing small-order alternatives

[Packlane's official product-box FAQ](https://packlane.com/products/product-boxes) offers corrugated product boxes from one unit and cardstock product boxes from 25. Accordingly, the pitch must not say that small brands cannot purchase packaging at all. Product, production method, quality, finish and timing determine which constraint matters.

## CommonGround's target

Brands considering the same underlying product format can compare supplier-permitted configurations, identify the conditions that prevent an order, and explicitly approve a change in budget, quantity allowance or physical finish before each final quote and payment.

The current demonstration deliberately retains its fictional kraft-box catalog: 100 units per base, seven production days, and synthetic USD prices. The Packhelp rigid box above is a different product. It is a grounding example, not a replacement or validation of that catalog.

## Evidence still needed for an actual supplier pilot

- Exact internal/external dimensions, construction, board grade and tooling identity.
- Whether separate brand artwork can share a production minimum; separate label/sleeve minima and setup charges.
- Order increments, per-unit charges, tax, delivery and accepted payment/cancellation terms.
- Supplier confirmation of the complete proposed specification and production schedule.

No supplier contact, quotation request, purchase or partnership has been made as part of this review. Shared-production capability remains unverified.
